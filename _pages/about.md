# About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Geography and Environmental
Management at the University of Waterloo, supervised by Dr. Derek T.
Robinson.

My research focuses on **urban forestry, remote sensing, UAV LiDAR,
tree allometry, and 3D point-cloud analysis**.

My current research investigates the use of remotely sensed tree
height and canopy dimensions to estimate diameter at breast height
(DBH), with the broader goal of supporting urban tree biomass and
carbon estimation.

## Research Interests

- Urban forestry
- UAV / RPA LiDAR
- Remote sensing
- Tree allometry
- Height–diameter relationships
- Urban tree biomass and carbon
- 3D point-cloud analysis

## Current Research

My PhD research investigates methods for estimating urban tree
structural attributes from high-resolution LiDAR data.

A major focus of my work is developing and evaluating models that use
tree height, canopy width, species, and other structural or
environmental characteristics to predict DBH.
