# Research

My research focuses on using remotely sensed measurements to
characterize individual urban trees and improve urban forest
inventories.

## Urban Tree Allometry

I investigate relationships among diameter at breast height (DBH),
tree height, canopy width, species, and environmental characteristics.

My research examines whether remotely sensed structural variables can
improve conventional height–diameter models for urban trees.

## UAV LiDAR

I use high-density UAV LiDAR point clouds to derive individual-tree
structural measurements including:

- Tree height
- Canopy width
- Canopy area
- Point-cloud density

## 3D Remote Sensing

I also compare LiDAR and structure-from-motion photogrammetry for
measuring individual urban trees.

## Long-term Goal

My goal is to develop transferable approaches that allow UAV surveys
to estimate DBH and subsequently support biomass and carbon estimation
across urban landscapes.
